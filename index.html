Project 01 · Account Health Score

An honest health score for every customer

One 0 to 100 score per customer, rebuilt every morning, with AI-written next steps in Salesforce.

SnowflakeSQLCortex AISalesforce

Problem

Customer health lived in hand-entered CRM fields. They were opinions, updated rarely, and by the time an account looked at risk it was usually too late to act.

Solution

I built one score for every customer from 13 measures across Finance, Tech Support and Services. Each measure is marked 1 to 5 and weighted by area. Override rules cap the score when any area is red and flag priority accounts before renewal. Then, for every score, AI writes a Health Next Steps field from the account's actual records: up to three problems, each with why it matters, the likely cause and the next step, naming who should act and which record to open. It lands in Salesforce next to the score, with a reason for every measure, so client managers see what to do where they already work.

Result

Tested against four years of history, the score would have warned about most of the customers we lost, well before they left.

70%of lost customers were red beforehand, vs about 30% under the old bands
5×red accounts were lost about five times as often as green ones
98%of recurring revenue covered by the score

Project 02 · Revenue Retention

Retention numbers leadership can trust

ARR, NRR and GRR rebuilt from contract lines, with a history that no longer moves.

SnowflakedbtStreamlitCortex Analyst

Problem

Retention came from legacy reports that double-counted product lines and called a month-over-month ratio "retention". Recurring revenue was overstated by 29%.

Solution

I rebuilt ARR from contract lines and froze every closed month in a snapshot, so history stops shifting. The dashboard shows the ARR bridge (new, expansion, contraction, churn), 12-month NRR and GRR, and every client's movement, with AI chat built in.

Result

Leadership now reads one retention number that ties back to source, row by row.

29%overstatement in the old reports removed
100%of rows reconciled back to source, to the cent
±5%restated history stays within 5% of Finance-approved figures

Project 03 · Ask the Data

Answers in seconds, not days

An "Ask the data" chat in every dashboard, grounded in a semantic view and checked before it runs.

Cortex AnalystSemantic viewsStreamlitJira

Problem

Simple questions waited in a queue. People could spend days hunting for one number, or ask the data team and wait.

Solution

Every dashboard has an Ask the data button. A plain-English question goes to Cortex Analyst over a semantic view written for that dashboard. A SQL guard checks every table the query touches before it runs. The answer comes back as a number, a chart or a table, with the SQL one click away. A thumbs-down asks why and files a Jira ticket.

Result

People answer their own questions inside the dashboard they already use, and every wrong answer becomes a fix.

Secondsto answer common questions, instead of days
100%of AI-written SQL checked against an allow-list before it runs
~30 sfrom a thumbs-down to a Jira ticket

Project 04 · Data Platform & Customer Mart

A dozen systems, one governed model

The data platform under every dashboard, and the customer mart that joins CRM and ERP data.

SnowflakedbtFivetranSalesforceNetSuite

Problem

Business data lived in about a dozen systems: CRM, ERP, HR, ticketing and project tools. Every report rebuilt its own joins, and teams disagreed on what a "customer" was.

Solution

I brought the systems into Snowflake with Fivetran and built dbt star schemas on top: a Salesforce and NetSuite customer mart with shared dimensions, curated views for each dashboard, and semantic views that define every metric once. Scheduled tasks freeze snapshots and keep everything fresh.

Result

One model that every dashboard, report and AI answer reads from.

99%pipeline success rate over 20,000+ scheduled runs
99%faster pages after moving heavy queries to scheduled tables (100 s to under 1 s)
1definition of a customer, shared by CRM and ERP data

Project 08 · AI Spend

Every AI dollar in one place

Spend across the AI tools we use, by provider, product, person and model. Snowflake Cortex is live; OpenAI and AWS Bedrock are next.

SnowflakeSQLStreamlitSemantic views

Problem

AI use was growing across several tools, each with its own bill and console. Nobody could say what we spend on AI in total, who drives it, or whether it is rising. Even inside Snowflake, reading the usage views live took about 100 seconds per page.

Solution

I built an hourly pipeline that copies usage into Snowflake, prices it at the billed rate for each service and day, and keeps one source per product, so nothing is counted twice. The dashboard shows spend by product, interface, person and model, with month to date, a month-end projection and an Ask the data chat. Today it covers Snowflake Cortex: CoCo and every other Cortex AI service. Next, OpenAI and AWS Bedrock usage lands in the same model, so the same pages show every provider side by side.

Result

Managers see total AI spend, who drives it and where it is heading in one place, and every number ties back to source.

<1 spage loads, down from about 100 s reading usage views live
100%of headline numbers matched direct SQL at sign-off
Hourlyrefresh; usage lands 1 to 3 hours after it happens
